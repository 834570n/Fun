Redline Circuit

A 1,250 kg rear-drive coupe with 207 Nm, a six-speed box and a limited-slip diff on a 2.5 km circuit. Tyres, weight transfer, wheelspin and lock-ups are simulated at 1,000 steps per second.

Hold your phone sideways. Drag anywhere on the left half to steer. The pedals are analog: touch higher on a pedal to press it harder. The four bars under the speedo show tyre grip (green has margin, yellow is at the limit, red is sliding).
